1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish requirements for controlling access to Northwind information systems in order to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of company and customer data.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors and third parties who access Northwind systems, applications, networks and data, whether on premises or remotely.

3. Definitions

Privileged account: an account with administrative rights on a system.

4. Policy

4.1 User accounts

  • Each user must be assigned a unique user ID; shared accounts are prohibited.
  • Access must be approved by the system owner before it is granted, based on least privilege.

4.2 Passwords

  • minimum length of 8 characters
  • Passwords should be changed regularly.

4.3 Multi-factor authentication

MFA must be enabled for all remote access and for all administrator accounts. This requirement supports ISO 27001 Annex A 8.5 and NIST AC-2.

4.4 Leavers

IT will disable accounts of terminated employees within 5 business days of their departure.

4.5 Access reviews

Managers shall review user access every 6 months. Privileged access shall be reviewed quarterly.

5. Roles and Responsibilities

RoleResponsibility
CISOOwns this policy and approves exceptions.
IT OperationsProvisions and removes access as approved.
Line ManagersApprove access requests and complete access reviews.
All UsersProtect their credentials and report suspected misuse.

6. Compliance

Compliance with this policy will be monitored by Internal Audit. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Exceptions must be approved by the CISO.

7. References

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A 5.15, 5.18; NIST SP 800-53 AC-2, IA-5; CIS Control 6.
