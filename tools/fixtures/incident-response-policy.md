# Incident Response Policy

**Owner:** Head of Security Operations
**Effective date:** 2025-01-10
**Review:** every 12 months

## Purpose
This policy defines how the organization detects, reports, contains and recovers from information security incidents to minimise harm.

## Scope
Applies to all staff, contractors and service providers, and to all systems and data.

## Policy statements
- All suspected incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Center within 1 hour of discovery.
- Incidents must be triaged and assigned a severity within 4 hours.
- Personal data breaches must be notified to the supervisory authority within 72 hours where required by GDPR Article 33.
- A post-incident review must be completed within 10 business days for SEV1 incidents.
- The incident response plan shall be tested annually through a tabletop exercise.

## Responsibilities
- Incident Commander: leads the response to SEV1 and SEV2 incidents.
- SOC: monitors alerts 24x7 and coordinates response.

## Enforcement
Failure to report an incident may lead to disciplinary action.
